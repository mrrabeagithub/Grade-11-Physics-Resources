Reference Information & Formulae

Constants & Conventions

  • Gravity: g = 10 m/s²
  • Sign Convention: Counter-clockwise (CCW) is positive (+). Clockwise (CW) is negative (-).
  • Equilibrium Condition: ΣM = 0
  • Net Moment: ΣM = Iα

Moments of Inertia (I)

  • Point Mass:
    I = mr²
  • Solid Disk/Cyl:
    I = ½MR²
  • Thin Rod (Center):
    I = 1/12 ML²
  • Thin Rod (End):
    I = 1/3 ML²
  • Solid Sphere:
    I = 2/5 MR²
  • Hoop/Ring:
    I = MR²
